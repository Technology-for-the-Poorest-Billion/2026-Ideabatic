| Issue Encountered | Mitigation |
|------------------|------------|
| Temperature sensors were unavailable at the start of the project, delaying the planned testing schedule. | A contingency plan was implemented by sourcing alternative sensors and adjusting the project timeline. Once testing showed that temperatures stabilised after approximately 5–6 hours, shorter tests could be conducted, allowing progress despite the delay. |
| Only one cooler bottle was available for testing, and replacement bottles were out of stock. | Testing schedules were carefully planned to maximise use of the available bottle. Time was used efficiently between freezing and testing periods to minimise delays and increase the number of experiments conducted. |
| Communication through text messages was sometimes ineffective for discussing technical ideas and project decisions. | The frequency of in-person meetings was increased to improve communication, facilitate faster decision-making, and ensure all team members remained aligned. |
| Early issues were encountered with file storage, version control, and document accessibility across the team. | Google Drive was adopted as a centralised platform for file storage, sharing, and collaboration, improving accessibility and organisation. |
| Hot-glued sensor interfaces did not always produce a reliable watertight seal, leading to the risk of leakage during testing. | An iterative sealing process was adopted, involving repeated application of hot glue followed by seal verification tests. Multiple testing stages were used to confirm seal integrity before experiments were conducted. |
| Initial cooler insert designs did not fit optimally within the bottle, particularly around the threaded neck section. | The inserts were redesigned to better match the bottle geometry and accommodate the lid closure mechanism, improving fitment and allowing the hot-glued interfaces to be sealed more effectively. |
